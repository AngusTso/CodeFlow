About this project

CodeFlow is a prototype exploration, not a production AI video-generation service. AI and media-generation actions may be simulated to demonstrate intended interaction flows. The demos are provided as standalone HTML files.

Review focus: interaction and frontend design, responsive behavior, information architecture, and the trade-offs between three different creative workflows.
